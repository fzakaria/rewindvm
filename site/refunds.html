You get a full refund within 30 days of purchase, for any reason. Email tacos@lunchtimesurf.com from the email address you bought with, or tell us that address, and we will take care of it. You do not need to explain why.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up on your statement.

A refunded license key is deactivated. License keys are checked offline, so a copy of the app that is already installed is not switched off remotely, but the refunded key stops being valid for updates and for new installs.
