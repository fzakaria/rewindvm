Rewind VM

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026.

Rewind VM is made by Lunch Time Surf LLC, a California limited liability company based in Santa Cruz ("we"). This policy covers this website, the Rewind VM engine and command line, and the Rewind VM desktop app.

This website

rewindvm.dev is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google see your IP address and browser details as with any web request, and their own privacy policies apply to that.

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see what people use: which pages they read, where they came from, whether they reach the pricing and download sections, which Buy button they press, which install command they copy and whether a visit ends in a purchase. To tie a purchase to its visit, the Buy buttons pass Stripe the random visitor id Google Analytics uses, and nothing else about you. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell one visit from the next. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it sets no cookies and sends Google only a cookieless notice of each page view. Google's advertising features are turned off, so none of this is used for ads. Google processes the data under Google's privacy policy. A content blocker that blocks Google Analytics leaves the site working as before.

Only the website does this. The engine, the command line and the desktop app send nothing.

Your runs stay on your machine

The engine, the command line and the desktop app run on your computer. Your workloads, the inputs they run on, their keyframes, forks and anything you export stay on your machine. We never receive them. They leave your machine only when you share them yourself, for example by attaching an exported run to an issue.

The desktop app works offline

The app does not phone home. There are no accounts, no telemetry and no crash reporting that sends data to us. License keys are checked on your machine, offline, so entering one sends nothing anywhere.

Buying a license

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives your email address, your billing details and your card to take the payment and send your receipt, under Stripe's privacy policy. We never see your card number.

From Stripe we receive your name and email address. We use them to email you your license key, to resend it if you lose it, to handle a refund, and to answer support requests. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share your details.

Email

If you email us, for early access or anything else, we keep the conversation to reply to you. Nothing more is done with it.

What Rewind VM does not do

  • No accounts.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking in the engine, the command line or the app.
  • No ads.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
